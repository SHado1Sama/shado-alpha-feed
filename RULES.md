# Rules for what becomes a signal

1. Title gate: an auto-fetched item only becomes a signal if a field keyword from config/rules.json appears in its title, as a whole word. This is the same fix the water opportunities board needed after substring matching let weak matches through.
2. Privacy first: Privacy carries a deliberate scoring boost. AI produces far more news volume, and without the boost the board, and then the brand, would drift into a generic AI feed.
3. Original source or nothing: every signal links to where it came from. Aggregator-of-aggregator links do not count when the original is reachable.
4. Early beats loud: a project announcement, release, listing notice or mint opening outranks commentary about it. Commentary only makes the board when it reveals something new, like the 138 million agent payments report.
5. Content test: if a signal cannot plausibly become a post, a thread, or a useful reply for @dak_shado, it does not belong on the board, however interesting it is.
6. No financial advice surface: no price targets, no buy or sell calls, no guaranteed returns, in titles, summaries or angles.
7. Dedupe: by normalised URL plus title together. One story, one card, best source wins, but distinct stories that share one source report URL all stay.
8. Freshness: auto signals older than 14 days drop off. Manual sweep signals stay until a sweep retires them.
9. Rejections stay off the board: scams, fake giveaway accounts and unverified airdrop bait are excluded silently. A warning post is Muse sweep work, not a board card.
