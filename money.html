🛡️ Money rules

Never pay to apply

Any program asking for an entry fee, a deposit, or a token purchase before you can earn is rejected from this board on sight.

Verify on the official page

Rewards and deadlines here come from the listing or program page at check time. Programs change terms quietly, confirm before you invest hours.

Disclose paid work

Paid posts and ambassador content should be disclosed as paid or partnered. It protects the page's trust, which is the asset everything else is built on.

Contests pay winners only

Bounties are competitions. Enter ones where your niche gives you a real edge, and reuse the research as regular content either way, so the work is never wasted.
